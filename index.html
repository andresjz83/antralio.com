Questions

What teams ask before their pilot.

Leases, rent rolls, CIMs, subscription documents, capital calls, side letters, board packs, accounting exports, broker pitches — any structured or semi-structured document or system your team processes repeatedly. PDFs and scans, spreadsheets, and direct connections to accounting and CRM systems.
Your data is fully isolated from other firms — never pooled, never shared. We never use your data to train AI models. We integrate with your existing SSO (Okta / SAML) for granular access control across your team and operating partners.
Every extracted value links back to its source — page number, clause, cell reference. Your analysts verify by clicking, not by re-reading entire documents. Disagreement on any field is logged and used to tune the extraction without retraining on your data.
We price per deployment — flat annual fee, no per-document metering, no per-seat charges. A 6-week paid pilot precedes every engagement so both sides can walk away if the accuracy bar isn’t met.
