Frequently Asked Questions

What document types does it handle?

Leases, rent rolls, subscription documents, CIMs, financial statements, side letters — any structured or semi-structured document your team processes repeatedly. If it comes in as a PDF, scanned image, or spreadsheet, we can extract from it.

Is our deal data secure?

Your data is fully isolated from other firms — never pooled, never shared. We never use your data to train AI models. We integrate with your existing SSO (Okta/SAML) for granular access control across your team and operating partners.

What if the extraction gets something wrong?

Every extracted value links back to its source — page number, clause, cell reference. Your analysts verify by clicking, not by re-reading entire documents.
